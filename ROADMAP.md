## Quality goals

Every portfolio artifact should answer:

1. What was the objective?
2. What environment and authorization boundary were used?
3. What was actually performed?
4. What evidence was collected?
5. What did the result mean?
6. What defensive/detection lesson follows?
7. What remains uncertain?
