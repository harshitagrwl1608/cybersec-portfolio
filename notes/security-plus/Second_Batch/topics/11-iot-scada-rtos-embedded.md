# IoT, SCADA/ICS, RTOS and Embedded Systems
**Source provenance:** Handwritten lecture notes from Professor Messer’s free CompTIA Security+ **SY0-701** training course.
**Professor Messer course alignment:** 3.1 – Architecture Models → Other Infrastructure Concepts (IoT / Embedded / RTOS / ICS)
**Coverage:** source pages 25–26

## IoT
IoT connects sensors, smart devices, and automation systems to provide everyday services. The notes warn about weak/default credentials and the reality that many IoT devices are not managed with enterprise-grade security.

## SCADA / ICS
SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) and industrial control systems can:
- Operate across multiple sites.
- Control large machinery.
- Be used in power generation and manufacturing.
- Provide real-time information and control.
- Require strong segmentation.

The notes also mention distributed control systems (DCS) and their real-time information/control functions.

## RTOS
A real-time operating system provides deterministic scheduling and aims to avoid waiting behind unrelated processes. The notes associate RTOS environments with:
- Very critical systems.
- High sensitivity to security problems.
- High availability requirements.
- A strongly self-contained design.

## Embedded systems
Embedded hardware/software is designed for a specific purpose or as part of a larger application. The notes use a **traffic light** as the example and emphasize efficiency and purpose-built behavior.
