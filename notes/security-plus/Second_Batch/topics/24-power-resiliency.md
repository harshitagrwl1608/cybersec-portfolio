# Power Resiliency
**Source provenance:** Handwritten lecture notes from Professor Messer’s free CompTIA Security+ **SY0-701** training course.
**Professor Messer course alignment:** 3.4 – Resiliency and Recovery → Power Resiliency
**Coverage:** source pages 66

## Power as foundation
Infrastructure does not work without electricity. Organizations may not control the primary power source and therefore use short- and long-term backup strategies.

## UPS — Uninterruptible Power Supply
The notes describe UPS protection for:
- Blackouts.
- Power surges.
- Low-power/brownout conditions.

Types listed:
- **Office/standby UPS.**
- **Line-interactive UPS.**
- **Online/double-conversion UPS.**

The notes associate online/double-conversion UPS designs with many features and higher-cost/high-assurance environments.

## Generators
Generators provide electricity but take time to start, so they are commonly paired with UPS systems: the UPS bridges the startup gap, while the generator supports longer outages.

## Operational principle
A resilient power design normally combines immediate ride-through (UPS) with longer-duration supply (generator or alternate source).
