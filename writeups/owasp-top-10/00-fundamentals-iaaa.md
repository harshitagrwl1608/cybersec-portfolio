# OWASP Top 10 — Fundamentals
 
## What the Top 10 Actually Groups
The OWASP Top 10 categories are groupings of vulnerabilities related to
failures in how **Identity, Authentication, Authorization, and
Accountability (IAAA)** are implemented in an application.
 
## IAAA
A simple way to think about how users and their actions are verified on
applications. **It is not possible to skip a level** — each one builds on
the one before it.
 
### i) Identity
A unique account representing a person or a service.
 
### ii) Authentication
Proving that identity (e.g. a password).
 
### iii) Authorization
What that identity is allowed to do.
 
### iv) Accountability
Recording/alerting on who did what, when, and where.
