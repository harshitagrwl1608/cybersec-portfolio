# Risk, GRC & Resilience

## Risk
**Risk = likelihood × impact**

Responses: **Avoid · Mitigate · Transfer · Accept**

Inherent risk = before controls.  
Residual risk = after controls.

## Key terms
Asset = valuable resource.  
Threat = potential cause of harm.  
Vulnerability = weakness.  
Exploit = method/code using a vulnerability.  
Risk = potential loss when a threat exploits a weakness.

## Governance
Policy = mandatory management direction.  
Standard = mandatory specific requirement.  
Procedure = step-by-step implementation.  
Guideline = recommended practice.  
Baseline = minimum secure configuration.

## BIA / continuity
BIA = determines business impact and criticality.

**RTO:** target maximum time to restore service.  
**RPO:** target maximum acceptable data loss measured in time.

High availability = keep service running.  
Disaster recovery = restore after disruption.  
Business continuity = keep essential business functions operating.

## Backups
Full = all selected data.  
Incremental = changes since previous backup.  
Differential = changes since last full backup.

**3-2-1:** 3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite.

## Sites
Hot = near-ready.  
Warm = partly ready.  
Cold = basic facility/resources.

## Third-party risk
**Due diligence → contract/SLA → requirements → monitoring → reassessment → exit strategy**
